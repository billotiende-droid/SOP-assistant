## Purpose

This procedure explains how employees submit purchase requests.

## Required Information

Every purchase request must include:
- Business purpose
- Item or service description
- Quantity
- Estimated cost
- Requesting department

## Review Process

The Procurement Process Owner reviews submitted purchase
requests and directs employees to the appropriate next step.
